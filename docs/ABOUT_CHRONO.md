# About Chrono (upstream)

This is a plain summary of the original app this build is based on. The original README is kept in the upstream repository: <https://github.com/vicolo-dev/chrono>.

Chrono is a free and open source clock app for Android, made by Vicolo and contributors and licensed under GPL-3.0. It is available on F-Droid as `com.vicolo.chrono`. Upstream describes the app as usable but still a work in progress, and asks users to test it on their own phone before relying on it for important alarms.

## Features

Alarms:
- Schedules: once, daily, on chosen week days, on specific dates, or over a date range
- Melody, volume, rising volume, vibration and audio channel per alarm
- Snooze length, maximum number of snoozes, and rules for what is allowed while snoozed
- Delete alarms automatically after they ring or finish, and skip the next occurrence
- Tasks that must be completed to turn the alarm off: math problems, retyping text, repeating a sequence, and others
- Dial, spinner and text time pickers
- Filtering, sorting and tags

Clock:
- Customisable clock display
- World clocks with the time difference to your location
- City search

Timer:
- Several timers at once
- Melody, rising volume and vibration
- Presets, full-screen view, dial and spinner duration pickers
- Filtering, sorting and tags

Stopwatch:
- Laps with lap time and total time
- Fastest, slowest, average and previous lap comparison

Appearance and other:
- Material You colours, custom colour and style themes, dark mode
- Home screen clock widgets
- Backup and restore of settings, alarms and timers
- Translations through Weblate

## Platforms

Android only. The alarm and timer code depends on Android.
